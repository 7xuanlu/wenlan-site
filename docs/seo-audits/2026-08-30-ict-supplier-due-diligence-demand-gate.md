# ICT supplier due-diligence evidence-pack demand gate

Captured: 2026-08-30

## Decision

The trilingual candidate passes the demand, clean-gap, product-proof,
standalone-utility, internal-link, and authority-path gates. It is a research
candidate only: no website URL is prepared or published by this record.

The task is a source-bounded ICT/software-supplier due-diligence evidence pack
for procurement, security, and IT owners. The trigger is that approved vendor
documents exist but their provenance, currentness, evidence gaps, and next
review are unclear. The output records source provenance, data-access scope,
resilience evidence, foundational security evidence, dependencies, gaps,
unanswered questions, an owner, and a review date.

This is not a certification or legal verdict. The candidate does not promise
certification validation, legal or privacy advice, vendor discovery, crawling,
live monitoring, vulnerability scanning, automatic scoring, approval or
rejection, or handling material outside approved controls.
