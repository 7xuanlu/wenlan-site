## Candidate contract

- Audience: proposal or capture leads and independent consultants who already
  possess a bounded, text-extractable RFP, RFQ, tender, or proposal brief plus
  factual source documents they are allowed to use.
- Trigger: before drafting, the reader needs to separate the buyer's exact
  words from internal assumptions, conflicting source material, unanswered
  questions, and proof that might later support a response.
- Task: build a local proposal-readiness evidence pack. Preserve exact source
  passages, document revision, assumptions, contradictions, open questions,
  and candidate proof points for human review.
- Outcome: a traceable reading and handoff artifact before writing begins. A
  human remains responsible for requirements, interpretation, completeness,
  bid or no-bid, scoring, pricing, approvals, and submission.
- Exclusions: no automatic extraction, scoring, recommendation, compliance
  validation, tender submission, current pricing, certification verification,
  legal advice, role-based workflow, or portal integration.
