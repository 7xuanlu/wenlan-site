- Audience: independent consultants, boutique consulting teams, and research analysts delivering client engagements.

- Task: build and maintain one client-project knowledge base without mixing clients or losing source support.
- Outcome: start the next deliverable from current traceable client knowledge and leave a handoff another consultant can reproduce.

## Product and safety boundary

The guide may claim user-held Sources, client-scoped Spaces, source-backed Pages, citations, refresh and review state, and documented plugin or MCP commands.

It must not claim CRM ingestion, email or calendar sync, automatic confidentiality classification, role-based access control, redaction, billing, project management, legal compliance, or cross-client sharing. Start with non-sensitive sample data; local-first storage does not replace contractual or security controls.
