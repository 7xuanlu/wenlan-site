## Decision and limits

- **Searcher and result:** a person with accumulated notes/files wants organized knowledge they can retrieve and use with AI. The homepage offers the existing product; specific setup questions remain on existing Learn pages.
- **Fit:** the current homepage already demonstrates imports, AI-organized pages, source inspection and connected AI tools. AI organization requires a configured model; user-edited pages remain reviewable. This is knowledge organization, not moving arbitrary files or recording meetings.
- **Candidate:** pairing “Personal Knowledge Management App” with “LLM Wiki” fits the existing product, but neither Reor's wording nor Obsidian's appearance proves the title or ordering is better. The exact combined title has no measured search volume, title-effect experiment, or acquisition lift. AI knowledge base remains in the description.
- **Why now:** this bounded title revision follows the user's explicit request to compare all three entry routes; it adds no article or feature claim. There is no causal test establishing it beats every alternative, nor proof this title alone resolves low authority/rank.

This supersedes both unshipped “Turn Notes and Files Into an LLM Wiki” and “AI Knowledge Base for Your Notes and Files” choices. The latter improved clarity but was not established as the best discovery category; the user's correction prompted the direct PKM-product comparison above. Existing GSC records 12 impressions / 1 click for exact `llm wiki` on the English wiki article, not the homepage. No evidence establishes the new title will outrank competitors or increase impressions; the decision is a better-supported targeting choice, not a measured growth result.
